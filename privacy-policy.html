This policy explains what personal data Bingua processes, why, on what legal basis, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and what rights you have.

The short version
  • You can use Bingua without an account. Your flashcards, progress and settings then stay on your device — we never see them. The one exception is your answers to the questions you are asked when you first open the app, which are included in our usage statistics (see below).
  • An account is optional and exists only so your learning data can be synced and restored. It requires an email address; if you use Google or Apple sign-in, your sign-in identity (which can include your name) is stored instead of a password.
  • Words and sentences you tap — taken from the video's public subtitles, not from anything you write — are sent to an AI provider to generate explanations. That provider is contractually forbidden from training its models on them, and does not store them at all.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics to see which features are used, including which words you tap or save and how you answered the first-launch questions. You can switch this off in Profile → Privacy.
  • If you send us feedback from the app (Profile → Send feedback), we store your message — and your email address, if you choose to give one — together with basic app and device details, so we can read it and reply. It is kept for 12 months.
  • We do not record your screen, use advertising identifiers, track you across other apps or websites, sell or share your data for advertising, or show ads.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app, at any time: Profile → Delete account.
  • Where your data lives: account data is stored in the United States (our database provider's region); usage analytics are stored in the European Union.

4. Videos and the YouTube player

Bingua uses YouTube API Services to show videos. Lessons play inside YouTube's official embedded player.

When a video loads, YouTube and Google receive the information any connection to their servers involves — including your IP address, device and browser characteristics — and the embedded player may store information on, or read information from, your device (for example cookies or similar identifiers set by YouTube inside the player). This happens under Google's own responsibility as an independent controller, and we neither control nor receive that data.

Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Your use of the embedded player is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Bingua itself does not request or receive any YouTube account data, and never asks you to sign in to Google in order to watch. We do fetch public information about videos — their titles and thumbnails — to display them in the app.

5. AI-generated explanations

When you tap a word, or open a sentence breakdown, Bingua sends that word or sentence — together with the surrounding subtitle line and its translation, all of which are public video content — to our servers, which forward it to Groq to generate the explanation. Your install ID travels with the request so we can rate-limit it and prevent abuse, and your account ID does too if you are signed in.

We never send anything you have typed — the request contains only subtitle text and the word you tapped.

Under our agreement with Groq:

  • Groq is contractually prohibited from using these inputs or outputs to train or fine-tune any model.
  • We have enabled Zero Data Retention on our Groq account, so Groq does not store the inputs or outputs of these requests at all — not even the limited window it would otherwise be permitted to keep them for troubleshooting and abuse investigation.
  • Processing takes place in the United States, under Standard Contractual Clauses.

Explanations are generated fresh each time and are not stored by us in a form linked to you. If you save a word or sentence as a flashcard, the explanation is saved into that card — which is your data, on your device and (if you have an account) in your account.

6. How long we keep things

Data kept until you remove it

Everything on your device — flashcards, preferences, streak, XP, saved videos, watch history — remains until you delete the app or use the reset function inside it.

Data kept until you delete your account

Your email address, all synced learning data, and any feedback you sent while signed in are deleted from our database when you delete your account (Profile → Delete account, or by emailing us). Copies may persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

Data that expires automatically

Rate-limiting records expire within 24 hours. Analytics events are deleted by our analytics provider after 12 months. Feedback you send from the app is deleted after 12 months; feedback sent as a guest is not linked to an account, so to have it deleted sooner, email us.

Data we keep after account deletion — and why

  • AI request counters. The counters that track how many AI explanations an install or account has requested are keyed only to the install ID or account ID and are retained indefinitely. Deleting them would reset the counters and make abuse of our AI services trivially repeatable, which is what they exist to prevent.
  • Video reports. Reports are moderation records about a video, not about you. They are retained, with any link to your account removed.

7. Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking exercises use your device's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your device and settings, your voice audio may be sent to Apple or Google for processing under their own privacy policies — this is the operating system's own service, not ours.

Bingua receives only the recognised text, compares it on your device to score your pronunciation, and then discards it. We never receive, store, or transmit your audio, and we never store the transcript. The microphone is active only while a speaking exercise is running, and only after you have granted permission, which you can revoke at any time in your device settings.

8. Analytics, and how to switch it off

We use PostHog (hosted in the European Union) to record a small, fixed set of product events — things like "a video was watched", "a quiz was completed", "the paywall was shown". Each event carries your install ID, your account ID once you sign in, and basic app and device context. PostHog derives an approximate country from your IP address.

We also record your answers to the questions Bingua asks when you first open it (the language you are learning, why you are learning it, your level, your topics, your daily time goal, your timeline, light or dark mode, and how you heard about Bingua), which of those questions you skipped, and any later changes to them in Profile. We keep your current answers on your analytics profile so we can compare, for example, which topics or levels lead to people sticking with the app. You choose every answer from a fixed list; none of them is free text. If you started using Bingua before version 1.5 of this policy, we do not collect your earlier answers or changes to them.

We deliberately do not collect: anything you type, the content of your notes, your email address, precise location, or any advertising identifier. We do not use session recording or screen replay. (Feedback you choose to send from the app is not analytics: it goes only to our own database, never to our analytics provider, and analytics records only that feedback was sent and its category — see §2.) We do not track you across other apps or websites, and Bingua contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

To switch analytics off: open Profile → Privacy and turn off Share usage data. While it is off, the analytics service is not started at all and nothing is sent from your device. This is also how you exercise your right to object under Article 21 GDPR; you can alternatively email us.

9. Automated decision-making and personalisation

Bingua personalises your video feed: it weights which videos to show you based on the topics you chose during onboarding, your stated level, and which videos you have already watched. This is profiling in the sense of Article 4(4) GDPR, and we want to be transparent about it.

It is not automated decision-making within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR: it produces no legal effects and nothing similarly significant — at most, a different video appears next. We do not carry out any Article 22 automated decision-making.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy;
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten") — the fastest route is Profile → Delete account;
  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances;
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests — including analytics, which you can switch off in the app;
  • Data portability — receive the data you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these, email sebabrockmann@gmail.com. We will respond within one month, as required by Article 12(3) GDPR.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens — autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. You may also complain to the authority in your own country of residence.

11. Deleting your account

Open Profile → Delete account in the app and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and all synced data — flashcards, preferences, usage, XP, saved videos and watch history — from our database. You can also request deletion by email.

Two things deletion does not do, so there are no surprises:

  • It does not cancel an active subscription. Manage subscriptions in your device's settings (on iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions).
  • It does not erase the data stored on your device. Use the reset function in the app for that, or delete the app.

12. Children

Bingua is not intended for children. In the Netherlands, the age at which a person can consent to online services on their own is 16. You must be at least 16 years old to use Bingua. We do not knowingly process personal data from anyone under 16; if you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Account data is protected by row-level access rules that restrict every record to its owner, passwords are stored hashed, and all connections use TLS encryption. Our AI, analytics and subscription providers are bound by data processing agreements. No system is perfectly secure — which is why the strongest protection here is that we collect as little as possible, and why the app works fully without an account at all.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Bingua develops. The version number and date at the top always identify the current version. If we make a material change — for example adding a new purpose or a new category of data — we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
